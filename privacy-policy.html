Privacy Policy for History Owner

Effective date: 2024-01-01

History Owner keeps selected sites out of your browsing history. We keep your data local and do not collect or sell personal information.

  • Data stored: only the domains you add to your block list, kept in Chrome storage (sync/local) to apply your preferences across sessions/devices.
  • Data collection/transmission: none. We do not send browsing data or personal info off your device.
  • Permissions use: `history`, and `storage` are used solely to detect visits to your blocked domains and delete those entries from history.
  • Remote code: none. All code ships with the extension and no code is fetched at runtime.
